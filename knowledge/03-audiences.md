## Two procurement models

This is the most useful way to segment public buyers:

- **Rural and smaller counties** buy informally and on relationships. One purchasing or finance contact often covers the whole county, including schools. Messaging: personal, direct, "call us and we will handle it."
- **Metro counties and larger cities** have formal procurement that requires competitive sealed bids or an approved cooperative contract. SWC 209 is the key differentiator here. Messaging: compliance, documentation, a defined process.

## Message by audience

- Chiefs and sheriffs: "Patrol-ready when it arrives."
- Purchasing and finance: "Compliant, documented, and easy to approve."
- Public works and utilities: "The right truck with the right body, spec'd correctly the first time."
- Small agencies: "Call one person. We handle the rest."
