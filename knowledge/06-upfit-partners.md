# Upfitting and partners

We do not install equipment in-house. We coordinate specialist upfitters and deliver a finished vehicle on one quote. That turnkey handoff is the main thing that separates us from a dealer who only sells the truck.

## What a turnkey patrol build includes

Taken from a recent four-unit Police Interceptor Utility build. Use this as the reference for what "patrol-ready" means:

- **Front:** push bumper with integrated warning lights, bumper and grille lights, siren speaker, headlight flashers
- **Cabin:** vehicle-specific console with printer mount, siren and light controller, power distribution with kill switch, armrest and cup holders, 54-inch roof lightbar, dual weapon mount with handcuff-key override
- **Prisoner area:** partition with sliding window, steel window barriers, cargo barrier compatible with side curtain airbags
- **Rear:** side-window, license-plate and rear warning lights
- **Other:** radio antenna and coax prep, wiring and expansion modules, installation labor, freight

Other upfit types we coordinate: service bodies, utility bodies, and equipment for fire, public works and administrative vehicles.

**Public materials stay brand-agnostic on equipment.** Say "lighting, siren, console, partition, cargo barrier, radio prep." Equipment brands, upfitter names and upfitter pricing are internal.
