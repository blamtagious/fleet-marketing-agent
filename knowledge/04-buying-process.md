# How buying works

The agency does one thing: approve the quote and issue a purchase order. We do the rest. This is the core story for all marketing.

## SWC 209 in plain terms

SWC 209 is the State of Tennessee's statewide contract for vehicles. Ford of Murfreesboro is an awarded dealer. The pricing was competitively bid by the state, so an eligible agency can buy from it as an approved cooperative contract instead of running its own bid. Pricing is published per model year by contract line (model, body code, trim package), with an MSRP and a contract price for each.

## The six steps

1. **Spec the vehicle (us).** We help the agency pick the right model, body code, wheelbase, package and upfit for the job.
2. **Quote (us).** One written quote: contract vehicle price plus the upfit, matched to the SWC 209 line.
3. **Approve and issue the PO (agency).** The only step the buyer owns.
4. **Order and track (us).** We place the factory order or pull a stock unit, then send build and delivery status updates.
5. **Upfit (us).** We schedule the upfitter, move the vehicle, and check the work.
6. **Deliver and invoice (us).** Finished vehicle, delivery sheet, and an invoice that matches the PO.

**Three things, one purchase.** Vehicle, upfit, paperwork. This phrase is already used in department materials and should stay consistent.

## Timing facts the agent should know

- Factory orders follow Ford's model-year order banks and build schedules. Lead times vary and are never guaranteed.
- Agencies buy on a fiscal-year budget (most Tennessee local governments run July 1 to June 30). Budget planning in spring and new-money purchasing in summer are natural campaign windows.
- Contract pricing changes with each model year. 2027 pricing is loaded; 2026 units may still be available.
