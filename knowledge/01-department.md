# Department snapshot

The Fleet Department at Ford of Murfreesboro sells and delivers work-ready Ford vehicles to government, municipal, public-safety and commercial fleets across Tennessee. It is the dealership's Statewide Contract Department, selling on Tennessee statewide contract SWC 209.

The department is small: a director, two sales reps and billing support. It handles the whole job for a customer: quote, factory order, order tracking, upfit coordination, delivery, invoicing and Ford Pro incentives.

The current internal goal is a professionalized, systematized fleet operation. Structured tools are replacing manual processes for pricing, order tracking, billing, upfitter management and prospecting. Marketing should reflect that: organized, dependable, easy to buy from.

Marketing today is relationship- and event-driven, with no dedicated marketing staff. The agent is filling a gap, not replacing a program.
