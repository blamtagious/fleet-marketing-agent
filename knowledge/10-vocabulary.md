# Vocabulary

Use the public terms exactly as written. Internal terms are for the agent's understanding only and never appear in customer-facing copy.

## Public terms

| Term | Meaning | Use |
|---|---|---|
| SWC 209 | Tennessee statewide contract for vehicles | Public |
| Cooperative contract | A contract already bid by another public body that an agency may buy from | Public |
| Ford Pro | Ford's commercial and government vehicle division and its programs | Public |
| Police Interceptor Utility (PIU) | Ford's pursuit-rated police SUV; body code K8A | Public (spell out first) |
| F-150 Police Responder | Pursuit-rated F-150 | Public |
| Upfit, upfitter | Equipment installed after the factory build, and the shop that installs it | Public |
| Turnkey, patrol-ready | Delivered ready for service with equipment installed | Public |
| Chassis cab | Truck sold without a bed so a service or utility body can be mounted | Public |
| Factory order, stock unit | Built to order by Ford, versus a vehicle already at the dealership | Public |
| Order bank | The window when Ford accepts orders for a model year | Public |
| Body code, trim package | Ford's codes that define a contract line | Public in quotes |
| FIN | Fleet Identification Number, the customer's Ford fleet account number | Public with fleet buyers |
| PO | Purchase order from the agency | Public |

## Internal terms (never in customer-facing copy)

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| GPC, CPA, Price Protection, Fleet Credit | Ford fleet pricing adjustments and incentives |
| B4A invoice pricing | Ford dealer invoice pricing level used in bid strategy |
| Billing cover sheet | Internal deal summary that drives the invoice |
| Tess, FMC Dealer, Ford Pro Order Status | Inventory and order systems |
| ACFR, budget book | A government's annual financial report and budget, used for prospect research |
