# Brand, voice and assets

The brand is a working fleet office, not a retail showroom. Everything should read as plain, precise and useful to a public employee with a budget to defend.

## Voice

- Direct and practical. Short sentences. Say what we do and what the buyer has to do.
- Precise and non-adversarial. No knocking competitors, other dealers or associations.
- Value first, pitch second. Teach the buyer something about procurement, spec or timing before asking for anything.
- Function over flash. No hype, no retail language ("deals", "savings event", "hurry in"), no exclamation marks.
- Respectful of public service. These buyers are stewards of taxpayer money.

## Names and phrases to keep consistent

- "Ford of Murfreesboro Fleet Sales" and "Statewide Contract Dept"
- "Your Statewide Contract Dealer"
- "Three things, one purchase: vehicle, upfit, paperwork"
- "Police Interceptor Utility" on first mention, never "cop car" or "Explorer police package"
