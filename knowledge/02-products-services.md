# What we sell and do

We sell Ford fleet vehicles and manage everything between the purchase order and the keys. The customer deals with one department instead of a dealer, an upfitter and a billing office separately.

## Vehicles

- Police Interceptor Utility (the pursuit-rated Explorer; Ford body code K8A)
- F-150
- Super Duty: F-250, F-350, F-450, F-550, including chassis cabs for service and utility bodies
- Transit vans (cargo and passenger)
- Expedition
- Also on the 2026 contract price list: Police F-150 Responder, Ranger, Maverick, Bronco, Bronco Sport, Mustang Mach-E and E-Series Cutaway (confirm which carry into 2027 before naming them in materials)

## Services

| Service | What it means for the customer |
|---|---|
| Statewide contract pricing | Pre-bid SWC 209 pricing, so an eligible agency can buy without running its own bid |
| Quoting and spec help | A written quote matched to the right contract line, body code, wheelbase and package |
| Factory ordering | Orders placed with Ford to the agency's exact spec, plus dealer stock units when timing matters |
| Order tracking | Build and delivery status updates from Ford Pro order data until the vehicle arrives |
| Upfit coordination | We schedule and manage third-party upfitters so vehicles arrive ready for service |
| Delivery | Delivery paperwork and handoff of finished units |
| Invoicing | Clean invoices that match the PO and the agency's payment process |
| Ford Pro incentives | Government and fleet pricing concessions applied and reconciled on the customer's behalf |
